# Claim wording matrix

This matrix constrains interpretation after results are observed. Wording may be weakened when required by the evidence but may not be strengthened beyond the listed boundary.

| Evidence or comparison | Allowed wording | Prohibited wording |
|---|---|---|
| Random-row or same-family row exposure | Same-state interpolation or a row-exposure diagnostic under the specified contrast | Independent cross-well generalization |
| Family-isolated source validation | Family-isolated transfer within the audited source setting | Cross-field or cross-basin confirmation |
| Matched split diagnostic with positive distortion | The specified within-family row exposure produced an optimistic performance shift on the fixed test observations | Row splitting causes the same average bias in all petroleum datasets |
| Matched dependence diagnostic with positive distortion | The nominal-file counterfactual produced an optimistic shift in the identified dependence case(s) | Petroleum wells generally have a dependence bias of the observed magnitude |
| Retrospective F-15 semantic analysis | Semantic inconsistency can manufacture catastrophic apparent transfer failure; this case motivated the frozen semantic protocol | A confirmatory estimate of the average semantic-bias magnitude |
| Prespecified semantic contrast with positive distortion | The specified semantic mismatch produced a pessimistic performance shift under the frozen contrast | The lower-error mapping is necessarily the correct physical interpretation |
| ExtraTrees better than SourceMedian under the frozen primary estimand | Feature-conditioned ROP information transported beyond the source cohort relative to a deployment-available source-only constant baseline | Deployment readiness, future forecasting, or causal optimization |
| ExtraTrees better than frozen Ridge | Nonlinear model complexity provided incremental external transport value under the frozen feature contract | Nonlinear models are universally required for ROP estimation |
| ExtraTrees better than SourceMedian but not Ridge | Learned conditional transport exists, but nonlinear complexity was not shown to be necessary | ExtraTrees is uniquely or generally superior |
| ExtraTrees not better than SourceMedian | The frozen evaluation provided little, no, or negative evidence of useful external conditional transport, according to the observed effect and uncertainty | The protocol failed merely because the model did not win |
| Retrospective Layer B | Retrospective external transport evidence | Confirmatory evidence |
| Locally frozen Layer C | Locally frozen external confirmation with the stated limitations | Externally timestamped or fully access-controlled confirmation |
| Layer D after all custody requirements pass | Externally timestamped, access-controlled multi-field confirmation in the sampled basin contexts | General random-basin population inference or deployment readiness |
| Positive results across several basin strata | Replication consistency across multiple sampled basin contexts | A population effect across all petroleum basins |

## Mandatory boundary statement

The task is depth-indexed, non-causal conditional ROP estimation. The fitted associations do not identify the causal effects of manipulating WOB, RPM, or standpipe pressure and must not be interpreted as a control or optimization policy.
